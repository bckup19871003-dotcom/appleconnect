    Helpful access.
    Clear boundaries.

    Remote support works best when you know who is helping, what they can see, and how to stop.

    Know your support person.

    Use someone you independently chose and contacted. An unexpected call, pop-up or message is not proof of identity. Confirm the purpose before opening an invitation or sharing a temporary session code.

    Share only what is needed.

    Close private apps and notifications. Do not share account passwords, bank verification codes, gift-card codes or full financial details. Pause or disconnect before entering anything sensitive.

    Approve, observe, disconnect.

    Review the app’s permissions and stay present. One-time guidance should not require permanent unattended access. You can decline control, ask for an explanation, or end the session.

    Need to stop?

    Use End session, Disconnect or Leave in the support app. On iPhone or iPad, also stop the screen broadcast. Closing this website alone does not disconnect a remote-support app.

    Less uncertainty.
    More clarity.

    Small details that make getting help easier.

    I am reading on my phone, but my computer needs help.

    Select the computer’s operating system in “Device needing support.” Open the selected official download link on that computer. The guide follows your manual choice; “Copy my setup” identifies both the reading device and the device you selected.

    I downloaded an app. Where did it go?

    Open your browser’s Downloads list or your device’s Downloads folder. Store apps normally appear in your app list. Match the file to the download you just started and the expected publisher. If the browser or OS blocks it, stop and ask your support person; do not disable device security to continue.

    Why can my support person see the screen but not control it?

    That can be expected. These iPhone and iPad flows share the screen while you make the taps. Supported Android apps on Chromebook are view only in the AnyDesk and TeamViewer receiving flows. Android control varies by device and add-on. Mac input control needs the relevant Accessibility approval.

    The Mac screen is blank, or a permission was declined.

    Ask which exact permission is missing. In System Settings → Privacy & Security, screen recording allows viewing; Accessibility may allow agreed input control. Names vary by macOS version. Reopen the app if prompted. Do not grant Full Disk Access or turn off protections just to follow this basic guide.

    This is a work or school device. Can I install the app?

    Your organisation may restrict downloads, app installs or screen sharing. Contact its IT team and use its approved method. You may need administrator approval. This site does not provide a way around those policies.

    Does submitting the form start a session or guarantee a refund?

    No. The form sends an enquiry to its owner. A remote session is a separate action in a support app. Payments, cancellations, account recovery and refunds are decided by the relevant provider; an independent support service cannot promise the outcome.

    Can I use this guide in the United States or Canada?

    Yes, you can read it in either region. App stores may display a local version, eligibility or language. Check the current listing, OS requirements and your organisation’s policy. The operator’s phone number is a US number; calling charges depend on your plan.

    Do I need an account, subscription or payment to receive help?

    It depends on the selected tool, provider and current app workflow. Do not create an account or buy a licence simply because a page offers one. Confirm any service terms and charges with the support operator first. This guide does not collect payments or provide a software licence.

    Keep the service healthy

    Setup is safe to run again in the same script project: it reuses the saved Form and spreadsheet, and does not overwrite a completed form’s later edits or republish it. Keep the Case Index name and columns intact. If you delete or recreate original questions, some standard index columns may be blank; all submitted answers are still retained in native responses and included in notification content. Share the Form and spreadsheet only with authorised staff; manage their access separately.

    The hourly check covers the last seven days and retries quota-deferred mail. “Sending” or “Review needed” requires owner review before retrying to avoid duplicate messages. Older missed responses can be recovered with the included manual function. No automated message is sent to the customer. Monitor notification quotas, execution failures and your inbox; maintain your own retention and deletion process.
